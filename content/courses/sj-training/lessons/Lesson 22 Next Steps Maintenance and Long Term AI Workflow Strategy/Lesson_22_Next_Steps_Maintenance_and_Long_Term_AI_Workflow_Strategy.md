# Lesson 22 - Next Steps, Maintenance, and Long-Term AI Workflow Strategy

## The Toolbar Is Never Truly Finished

Revit versions change. Office standards change. Users find new issues. That means your toolbar needs a maintenance strategy.

## Maintenance Checklist

- Review tools after Revit upgrades
- Keep release notes current
- Archive deprecated tools
- Test against sample projects
- Review user feedback monthly
- Keep AI context files updated

## Automation Backlog

Create a backlog with columns:

```markdown
| Idea | User | Impact | Risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rename views | Drafting team | Medium | Low | Planned |
```

## Measuring Value

Track:

- Time saved
- Errors reduced
- Users supported
- Manual steps removed
- QA consistency improved

## Final Reflection

Write a short summary:

- What did I build?
- What did I learn?
- What would I improve next?
- How can AI support the next version?

## ✅ Lesson Outcome

You leave with a long-term strategy for maintaining and expanding your pyRevit automation system.
