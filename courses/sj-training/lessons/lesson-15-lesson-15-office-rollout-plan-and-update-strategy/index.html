Lesson Content

Why Rollout Needs a Plan

Deployment is not just copying a folder. You need to consider testing, updates, user communication, and rollback.

Rollout Stages

1. Local development 2. Internal test model 3. Pilot user 4. Small team rollout 5. Wider department rollout

Update Strategy

Decide how users receive updates:

  • Manual copy
  • Shared network location
  • Git-based deployment
  • Managed IT process

Rollback Plan

Every release should answer:

  • What changed?
  • How do we revert?
  • Who supports it?
  • What Revit versions were tested?

💪 Simple Exercise

Write a rollout plan for your mini toolbar.

✅ Lesson Outcome

You can plan a controlled office deployment instead of pushing untested tools to everyone.
